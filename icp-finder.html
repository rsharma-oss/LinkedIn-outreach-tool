ℹ️ How these tiers are scored — and how to make them yours

Every connection is auto-classified by matching its job title and company against a keyword ruleset. The default is tuned for Shopify DTC growth agencies (founders & marketing leaders → T1, ecommerce tools & ecosystem → T2, broader marketing roles → T3). First match wins; anything that matches nothing is counted as "unmatched" in your totals (not dropped silently). Not your market? Tailor it to your business:
