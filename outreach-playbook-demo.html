Message Templates
Battle-tested templates for each situation. Always personalise the first line with something specific — a post, a company move, or a mutual connection.
T1 — First Messages (Decision Makers)
T1
Warm First Message — Short & Curious
Best for: new connections (0–14 days). Highest reply rate template.
▸
Hey [First Name] — noticed we just connected. I help [type of DTC/ecomm brand] drive more revenue from their existing customer base through email and retention. Would love to hear what you're focused on right now — what's the big initiative for [their company] this quarter?
💡 Keep it short. One question at the end. No pitch — you're just opening a conversation.
T1
Insight Opener — Share a Relevant Observation
Best for: when you have context from their posts, news, or company activity.
▸
Hey [First Name] — [saw your post on X / noticed [Company] just launched Y / read about your recent raise / noticed you're scaling fast into Z market]. That resonated. I work with brands at exactly that stage on [email/retention/DTC growth]. Happy to share what's been moving the needle for similar companies if it'd be useful. Either way, glad to be connected.
💡 The hook needs to be real — don't fake it. If you can't find something genuine, use the simpler template above.
T1
Warm Window Closing — Slightly More Direct
Best for: connections made 21–30 days ago where you haven't messaged yet.
▸
Hey [First Name] — we connected a little while back and I've been meaning to reach out. I help [type of DTC brand] with [retention/email/growth] — specifically [specific outcome, e.g. "reducing churn and growing LTV through smarter email flows"]. Is [outcome] something on your radar right now, or is the priority elsewhere?
💡 The question at the end is key — it invites a real answer rather than a yes/no. Even a "no, not right now" is useful intel.
Re-Engagement
RE-ENGAGE
Revive a Lapsed Conversation
Best for: contacts you spoke with 3–12 months ago but went quiet.
▸
Hey [First Name] — hope things have been going well since we last spoke. I've been heads-down on [something relevant you've been doing — a case study, a new service, a result you achieved for a client]. It made me think of our earlier conversation about [something specific from your last exchange if you have it / their situation]. Would love to reconnect — anything exciting happening on your end?
💡 Reference the previous conversation if you can. Even a vague "last time we spoke you were focused on X" shows you paid attention.
RE-ENGAGE
Timing Callback — "You Said When the Time Was Better"
Best for: contacts who explicitly said "not now" or "reach out later."
▸
Hey [First Name] — a few months back you mentioned [the timing wasn't right / you were mid-replatform / you had a lot on your plate]. I didn't want to let that hang forever. Is [situation] in a better place now? Happy to pick up where we left off, or just stay in touch if the timing's still not right.
💡 Only use this if you genuinely have the previous message context. Don't fabricate a reason — it reads as dishonest.
Follow-Ups
FOLLOW-UP
Follow-Up #1 — Add Value, Don't Just Bump
Send 5–7 days after first message if no reply.
▸
Hey [First Name] — just following up on my last message. Didn't want it to get buried. Thought this might be relevant: [one genuinely useful thing — a stat, a brief insight, an article, something that connects to their world]. No pressure — just wanted to put it on your radar.
💡 The "add value" part is mandatory. Don't just say "following up" — give them a reason to reply.
FOLLOW-UP
Break-Up Message — Leave the Door Open
Send 7–10 days after follow-up #1 if still no reply. Final message.
▸
Hey [First Name] — I know you're busy so I'll keep this short. If the timing's not right or this isn't relevant to what you're working on right now, completely understand. I'll leave the ball in your court — feel free to reach out whenever it makes sense. Hope things are going well on your end.
💡 The break-up message actually gets more replies than follow-up #1 — people respond to finality. Keep it warm, not passive-aggressive.
T2 — Ecosystem & Partnership
T2
Ecosystem Intro — Peer-to-Peer, Not Vendor
For T2 ecosystem contacts: tool vendors, agency peers, practitioners.
▸
Hey [First Name] — we're both in the [Shopify/DTC/email] space and I've been meaning to reach out. I run [Your Company] — we work with [type of brands] on [what you do]. Always looking to build relationships with people doing interesting work in the ecosystem. Would love to hear what you're seeing on your end — open to a quick catch-up sometime?
💡 Position yourself as a peer, not a vendor. The goal with T2 is relationship and referral, not a direct sale.
Outreach Cadence
A proven week-by-week rhythm for LinkedIn outreach. Consistency beats volume.

📌 The Rule of 5

Send 5 first messages per day, maximum. LinkedIn will throttle you if you send more than ~10–15 cold messages in a day — and quality suffers anyway. 5 thoughtful messages beats 20 copy-paste ones every time. With 8 urgent + 16 warm T1 contacts, you can work through all of them in one week at 5/day.
